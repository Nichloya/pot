**Tishaproject** is a my Sonic-romhack, which was released on SHC 2019. It was based on old version of [Sonic-Clean-Engine-S.C.E.-](https://github.com/TheBlad768/Sonic-Clean-Engine-S.C.E.-) (which based on Sonic 3 & Knuckles).

I worked on this hack from the end of 2018 until the fall of 2019. During the development process I acquired skills in working with [SonLVL](https://info.sonicretro.org/SonLVL) and [Flex 2](https://info.sonicretro.org/flex_2), which was very useful during the development of [Sonic-1-in-Sonic-3-S.C.E.-](https://github.com/TheBlad768/Sonic-1-in-Sonic-3-S.C.E.-) (Sonic 1 open source port to S.C.E. and S.C.E.-based templete for your hacks).

Tishaproject has collision and art bugs. Sometimes [[Tisha]] can fall through a loop or turn into visual trash and break the level's art.

> - [Unrealesed prototype 2020](https://www.mediafire.com/file/yzu4y0bpzkqbmwn/TishaProject.7z/file)

# SHC2019 description

While Sonic and Tails are resting, the Doctor Robotnik decided to act and take over the whole world with the help of a magical artifact. But Tisha found out about his plans, and decided to stop them from coming true.Can Tisha defeat the evil doctor and save the world? It all depends on you!

Play the current demo and help the Tisha!
